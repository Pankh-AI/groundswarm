Incident ID: MC-2026-0511-1. Starting at 03:14 UTC on 2026-05-11, Meridian Cache's edge nodes in the ap-south-1 region began serving stale content due to a cache-invalidation message backlog. Approximately 22% of read requests during the incident window received content older than the configured 5-minute freshness window. No requests failed outright; all returned 200 OK with stale bodies. The backlog was caused by a message-broker partition rebalance that paused invalidation delivery for 11 minutes. The incident was detected by an automated staleness monitor at 03:22 UTC and the broker partition was manually rebalanced at 03:27 UTC. Full cache freshness was restored by 03:41 UTC, a total incident duration of 27 minutes. No customer data was exposed or corrupted. Meridian Cache is adding a dead-letter alert for invalidation-queue backlogs exceeding 2 minutes and increasing partition count from 6 to 12 to reduce rebalance blast radius.
