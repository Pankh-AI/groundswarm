Honest finding: no mature open-source project combines both halves of Groundswarm's idea. The space splits into two clusters that don't overlap, swarm orchestration with no verification, and fact-verification research with no swarm scale, and nothing bridges them yet.

Cluster A — mature swarm orchestration, zero verification

kyegomez/swarms (7,043★, 986 forks, commits as recent as the day this was checked) and VRSEN/agency-swarm (4,520★, last push Aug 3) are active, well-adopted frameworks for running many concurrent agents. Neither ships any fact-checking, grounding, or fail-closed judging, it's pure orchestration plumbing (concurrency, topology, tool-calling), and leaving verification out appears to be a deliberate scope choice by the maintainers, not an oversight.

Cluster B — fact-verification multi-agent research, not swarms, mostly dormant

Academic "multi-agent debate for fact verification" work is the closest conceptual match: Tool-MAD (debaters each get a distinct retrieval tool, +35.5% over static-evidence debate) and FC-MAD / MAD-Fact use judge-guided consensus over 2–5 agents, but these are papers with no public repos.

None of these run more than a handful of agents. Adjacent but not a match: GPT Researcher / STORM run parallel research agents but treat "more sources scraped" as their accuracy mechanism, not an independent adversarial re-check. The 2026 "Cited but Not Verified" benchmark (also surfaced in the literature survey) found even leading deep-research products land at only 78–94% citation accuracy, a useful yardstick showing this problem is real and unsolved by well-funded labs, not a niche gap.

What Groundswarm could take from each

Borrow concurrency/isolation patterns from swarms/agency-swarm, they've solved running many workers reliably. Borrow the tool-augmented, judge-guided debate structure from Tool-MAD/FC-MAD for the verifier's internal design. But expect to implement published techniques from scratch, not adopt a library, since nothing production-grade exists to fork. This is genuine white space with no existing playbook: real differentiation, but real execution risk too.
