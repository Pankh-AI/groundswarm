Memory Landscape

Competitive check for the proposed Memory component · compiled 2026-08-13 · background research agent, model sonnet
Five specific design properties were checked against the existing agent-memory product and research landscape: validity/blast-radius scoping independent of authorship, a deterministic non-LLM pre-merge validator, review-gated high-stakes writes, tiered token-cost-aware loading, and explicit TTL/supersedes staleness. Twelve products and two 2026 research papers checked. No product or proposal combines all five. See ADR-007 for how this feeds back into the architecture.

The two closest research proposals

memorywire (arXiv 2606.01138, May 2026, single-author, unreviewed) specifies an optional human-in-the-loop governance channel with diff-and-approve workflows before writes enter long-term storage, the closest conceptual match to a review gate for high-stakes writes. But it's human review only, no deterministic invariant-checker, and it doesn't define org/team/project/run validity scoping.

"Don't Ask the LLM to Track Freshness" (arXiv 2606.01435, May 2026) argues explicitly that the field is under-serving deterministic, version-aware conflict resolution, direct evidence that researchers already see this as an open problem, not something quietly solved.

Why the deterministic-gate choice specifically matters

This is the sharpest finding: an independent 2026 benchmark on single-hop fact-conflict consolidation scored Mem0 at 18% and Zep/Graphiti at 7%, both rely on an LLM to decide whether a new fact supersedes an old one, and both fail on that task most of the time. This isn't a hypothetical argument for a deterministic gate over an LLM-trust model, it's a measured failure rate in the two most mature products checked. It directly supports the same design principle the literature survey found for the verifier (AbstentionBench: LLM judges don't reliably know when to decline), applied here to memory writes instead of task output.

Conclusion

No shipped product or well-known research proposal combines all five properties. Each piece has precedent somewhere, Letta's tiering, Graphiti's bi-temporal staleness model, LangGraph's TTL, memorywire's human-review channel, but nobody stitches them together, and the two products with the most mature consolidation logic (Mem0, Zep) both lean on LLM judgment for exactly the conflict-resolution step Groundswarm's design proposes to make deterministic, and both measurably struggle at it. That's a genuine, evidenced gap, not an inflated one. It is still an unbuilt proposal, not working code, see ADR-007 for status.
