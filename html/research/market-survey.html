Market Survey

Orchestration frameworks & verification/observability companies · compiled 2026-08-13 · two background research agents, model sonnet
Two questions: does any multi-agent orchestration framework ship real output verification, and does any evaluation/observability company verify a swarm's combined output against live re-fetched primary sources. Nineteen products checked (7 frameworks, 12 eval/observability tools). Answer to both: no.
Verification depth used below — (a) none no built-in verification · (a) none (b) LLM critic another LLM judges/reflects on output, no external re-check · (c) human gate human-in-the-loop approval primitive · (d) grounding checked against supplied or executable context, closest to Groundswarm's approach but never against independently re-fetched live sources

Headline finding

Across all 19 products checked, none perform independent verification of a multi-agent swarm's combined output against freshly re-fetched live primary sources. Every product's unit of evaluation is a single LLM call, a single agent's trace, or a single conversational turn, even tools marketed with "agent" or "multi-agent" language are tracing multiple steps inside one orchestrated run, not independently reconciling many workers' contributions into one fact-checked result.
