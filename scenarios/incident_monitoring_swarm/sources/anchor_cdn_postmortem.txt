Incident ID: AC-2026-0622-3. At 06:00 UTC on 2026-06-22, an automated TLS certificate for Anchor CDN's api.anchorcdn.net endpoint expired without being renewed, causing all HTTPS requests to that hostname to fail with certificate validation errors for 34 minutes. Roughly 100% of traffic to that specific hostname was affected; traffic to other Anchor CDN hostnames was unaffected. The renewal failure was traced to an expired API token used by the certificate-renewal automation, which had not been rotated since the automation was last updated. On-call engineers were paged by an external uptime monitor at 06:19 UTC, manually issued a new certificate at 06:28 UTC, and completed rollout to all edge nodes by 06:34 UTC. No data was lost; failed requests simply received connection errors and were retried by client SDKs. Anchor CDN is adding a 14-day-before-expiry alert for all renewal-automation credentials and moving certificate renewal to a credential-less workload-identity flow.
