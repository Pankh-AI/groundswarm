Incident ID: RA-2026-0715-1. At 20:02 UTC on 2026-07-15, Ridgeline Auth's session-refresh endpoint began returning elevated 500 errors after a database connection pool was exhausted by a misconfigured batch job that opened connections without releasing them. Approximately 6% of session-refresh requests failed during the 22-minute incident window; users with still-valid access tokens were not logged out, but token refresh for expiring sessions failed for affected users. The batch job was identified via connection-pool saturation alerts at 20:11 UTC and killed at 20:14 UTC, after which the connection pool recovered automatically within 10 minutes. No credentials or session data were exposed. Ridgeline Auth is adding a hard connection-count cap to all batch jobs and a separate, smaller connection pool reserved exclusively for the session-refresh endpoint.
