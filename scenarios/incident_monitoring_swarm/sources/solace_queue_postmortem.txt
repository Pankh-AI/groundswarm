Incident ID: SQ-2026-0703-2. Beginning at 11:45 UTC on 2026-07-03, message consumers for Solace Queue's order-events topic in the us-west-2 cluster began falling behind due to a slow downstream webhook receiver introduced in a partner integration the prior day. Consumer lag grew to approximately 340,000 unacknowledged messages at peak, delaying order-event delivery by up to 19 minutes for affected customers. No messages were dropped; the queue's retention window of 72 hours was never approached. Engineers identified the slow receiver via consumer-lag alerting at 12:03 UTC and throttled the specific partner's webhook concurrency at 12:11 UTC, which stabilized lag within 8 minutes. Full backlog drain completed at 12:52 UTC. Solace Queue is adding per-partner concurrency limits by default for all new webhook integrations and a consumer-lag alert threshold of 50,000 messages.
