Vendor A Object Storage — Incident Postmortem

Incident ID: VA-2026-0302-1
Date: 2026-03-02
Start time: 14:12 UTC
Region: us-east-2
Duration: 47 minutes
Severity: SEV-2

Summary
-------
Starting at 14:12 UTC on 2026-03-02, a subset of object storage requests in
the us-east-2 region began returning 403 Forbidden errors. The issue
affected approximately 8% of read requests and 3% of write requests in the
region. The incident was resolved at 14:59 UTC, a total duration of 47
minutes.

Root Cause
----------
An internal deployment pushed an updated access-control policy to the
regional authorization service. The policy contained a misconfigured
condition block that incorrectly evaluated a subset of bucket-level grants
as denied. The misconfiguration was introduced by a routine policy-sync job
and was not caught by pre-deployment validation because the validation
suite did not cover this specific condition-block interaction.

Impact
------
No data was lost or corrupted during the incident. Affected requests
received 403 Forbidden responses; no requests were silently dropped or
misrouted. Customers relying on default retry behavior in official SDKs
experienced elevated latency but no failed operations after retry.

Remediation
-----------
The misconfigured policy was identified at 14:51 UTC and a rollback to the
previous known-good policy was initiated at 14:53 UTC. The rollback
completed and traffic returned to normal at 14:59 UTC.

Commitments
-----------
Vendor A is extending the pre-deployment validation suite to cover
condition-block interactions of this type, and is adding a canary stage
to the policy-sync job that will limit any future misconfiguration to a
single availability zone before full-region rollout.
